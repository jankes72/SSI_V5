## Budgeting principle

Do not derive the grant budget from "how much money would be useful". Derive it from:

```text
TASK
-> OWNER
-> PERSON-MONTHS
-> FACILITY / EQUIPMENT NEED
-> TRAVEL / VALIDATION NEED
-> SUBCONTRACT / PURCHASE NEED
-> DELIVERABLE
-> JUSTIFIED COST
```

## Budget credibility checks

Before submission verify:

- person-months match the technical plan;
- WP lead has enough effort to lead;
- physical validation has actual facility/operator effort;
- cybersecurity/safety are not unfunded afterthoughts;
- dissemination/exploitation has owners and effort;
- data management and reporting effort exists;
- integration travel is realistic;
- external validation is not assumed to be free;
- compute/model costs have a documented basis;
- contingency is handled within programme rules.

## Funding should buy evidence

A strong SSI budget should visibly move maturity:

```text
INTERNAL SOFTWARE EVIDENCE
-> EXTERNAL HELD-OUT BENCHMARK
-> SIMULATION
-> CONTROLLED PHYSICAL PILOT
-> INDEPENDENT REPLICATION
```

If a cost item cannot be linked to this progression or required project management, its necessity should be challenged.

## Legal applicant boundary

Before proposal submission, confirm:

- which legal entity participates on the SSI side;
- ownership/licensing of SSI background to that entity;
- employment/contracting model;
- financial capacity and administrative responsibilities;
- VAT/tax/accounting treatment as applicable;
- who signs the grant and consortium agreements.

This document does not assume that the individual author personally qualifies as a beneficiary under a particular call.
