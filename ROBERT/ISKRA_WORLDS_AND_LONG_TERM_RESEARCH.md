# ISKRA Agents, Worlds and SSI's Long-Term Learning Programme

**Date:** 12 October 2026.  
**Project author and owner:** Paweł Jankiewicz (`jankes72`).  
**Scope:** individual ISKRA memory, S1–S40 and WEB training, Football World/Hermes, other domains and planned repair learning.  
**Claim boundaries:** existing components and results retain their own scopes. Completion of S40/WEB, long-term divergence between agents, subjective feelings and profitability are not assumed.

## Executive summary

### 1. Independent agents and persistent experience

The programme studies how separate, persistent agent histories affect competence selection, code generation, responses to failure, collaboration and new problem solving. Each ISKRA agent retains its own starting point, memory, skills and history. Experimental “zero” is a defined baseline, not a reset of the underlying model's weights. Robert's operational consolidation and the controlled ISKRA research cohorts remain distinct, with explicit records of any knowledge exchange.

### 2. Core training and visible artifacts

The published V4 result closes S1–S10 within its stated scope. S11–S40 continues the study of adaptation, review, repair and competence consolidation; full S40 completion has not been announced. A subsequent freeze is intended to preserve each actor's state before WEB01–WEB24. WEB will add inspectable websites, graphics and interfaces, supporting comparison of correctness, accessibility, implementation and recurring visual features.

### 3. Football data and teacher generations

Football World draws on the author's reported five years of data and tool development. Historical T17 contains 17 Hermes teachers in 12 + 5 families, with different models and data perspectives. A 60% model-training / 40% observation split supports knowledge collection about teacher behaviour. If agents see the observation outcomes during development, those data are not a blind final test. Teacher identity, knowledge generation and access time must remain traceable.

### 4. Original code and prospective evaluation

Agents are intended to develop their own code, select sources, group predictions, construct combinations and allocate stakes through the world's laboratories. Decisions and code are frozen before future outcomes are known. Returning agents retain their own histories and versioned Pocket materials. Historical evaluation, prospective evaluation without real stakes and actual financial outcomes are separate evidence categories; no profitability result is announced.

### 5. Repairs, new domains and long-term research

The planned repair curriculum uses archived defects and fixes to test diagnosis, correction, regression and transfer to unseen variants. Other worlds and company applications require their own data, adapters and acceptance tests. Affect-like indicators are studied as functional states that may influence learning and decisions; they are not evidence of subjective feelings or consciousness. Long-term reports preserve individual trajectories and the origin of acquired knowledge.

## Full documentation

The central research question is how separate, persistent histories influence agents' competence selection, code generation, handling of failure, collaboration and performance on new problems. Worlds provide domain data, teachers, laboratories and consequences; training supplies common reference points. Repeated trials are intended to trace development rather than compare a single answer.

### 1. Two connected development lines

Robert is SSI's continuing engineering agent. ISKRA agents are separate research actors with their own starting points, memory, competencies and histories. An initial “zero” state defines an experimental baseline. It does not mean resetting the underlying model's weights or erasing canonical Robert.

On 12 October, the operator reported connected bridges and consolidation of Robert's validated competencies into the collective. The primary ISKRA study preserves individual separation. An available tool library and an agent's personal experience are different resources.

An ISKRA agent may use a shared tool, receive material from another actor or collaborate on code. Each influence should retain authorship, version and access time. Conversation alone does not merge private memories. Incorporating a shared competence into an ISKRA agent's state is a separate, observable event.

Initial comparisons of independent learning and subsequent collaboration phases are kept separate. A shared repair programme may become a new stage for all ISKRA agents while preserving their earlier frozen reference states.

### 3. S1–S40 and developmental memory

The subsequent six drone scenarios and 15 humanoid scenarios are separate software-laboratory results. See the [published result](../RESULTS/V4_S1_S10_AND_POST_S10_RESULTS_20260917.md).

S11–S40 extends the problem-solving process: selection of micronetworks and Blocks, candidate construction, adaptation, review, experience of failure and controlled consolidation. Growth refers to measured competence state, memory, routing and history. It does not automatically imply retraining the base LLM's weights.

The publications preserve verified subsets of S20–S25, an interrupted S26 and subsequent limited retests. A new PASS does not erase an earlier FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE or establish completion of all S40 training. The [status index](../CURRENT_TRUTH_INDEX.md), [repair record](../AKTUALNA_NAPRAWA.md) and [programme lineage](../RESEARCH_PROGRAM_LINEAGE_AND_POST_S40_PLAN_20261004.md) define these boundaries.

### 4. WEB and comparison of visible solutions

WEB is intended to extend comparison to artifacts that can be preserved and rendered: websites, information layouts, graphics and interfaces. Evaluation covers correctness, required content, accessibility, responsiveness and implementation structure. Recurring layout, spacing, typography and colour features may be measured separately.

Each artifact should identify its author, task, parent S40 state, source version, renderer version and competencies used. A common template does not by itself establish an individual agent style. The primary independent-learning phase remains separate from subsequent consultation and transfer between actors.

The new domain allows examination of whether prior history affects reuse, exploration, stability, code diversity or recovery from error. Different results from every ISKRA agent are not assumed. Detailed criteria remain in the [S40/WEB protocol](../LONGITUDINAL_S40_WEB_AND_AFFECT_STUDY_20261004.md).

### 5. Football World and Hermes

Football World is Paweł Jankiewicz's multi-year programme of data, models and tools. The author describes approximately five years of preparation; this is a statement of work history, not a census of all records. Materials cover statistics, odds movements, representations and model comparisons, including Poisson, Dixon–Coles, Random Forest and neural networks. These established methods retain their respective origins.

Historical T17 comprises 17 teachers in 12 + 5 families. Their perspectives include full trends, odds changes, amplitude and rate of change, maximum deviations, raw and logarithmic starting/ending values, ratios, statistics, and prepared odds data with percentage changes. Each teacher contributes its own model, data preparation and observation memory. See the [T17 record](../evidence/FOOTBALL_WORLD_T17_TEACHER_SYSTEM.md).

#### Model training, observation and the knowledge collector

The historical contract allocates 60% of the data to model training and 40%, including outcomes, to observation of model behaviour. The collector retains information about classes, deviations, confidence, recurring errors and previous observations. Knowledge of when a teacher is useful is a resource distinct from that teacher's individual prediction.

If an agent uses outcomes from the 40% observation set while developing a solution, that set becomes development material for the agent. A separate sample unused in design, and future events, retain the testing role. Each experiment's data split requires its own identity and chronology.

#### Laboratories and original code

The world provides the following stages, each with required artifacts:

| Stage | Agent activity |
| --- | --- |
| Hermes selection | Obtain specified sources and knowledge generations, together with data and metadata |
| LAB1 SOLO | Develop features, datasets, a hypothesis, a program, a model or a strategy |
| LAB1 SHARED / collaboration | Exchange explicitly shared materials and conduct joint experiments while preserving authorship |
| LAB2 | Select and group prediction sets, and compare variants |
| LAB3 | Construct group combinations, ticket plans, stakes, costs and resource allocation |
| Freeze Gate | Freeze code, selections and rules before future outcomes are known |
| Outcome Hall | Link decisions to independently established real outcomes and settle results |
| Reflection / subsequent cycle | Analyse successes, errors and assistance, and retain experience after leaving and returning |

Generating original code and discovering new relationships are programme objectives. Whether an agent can produce a solution that the SSI author did not previously design remains an experimental question. Each particular result requires its own test.

#### Knowledge generations and each agent's Pocket

An agent retains the Hermes identity, knowledge generation, access time and selected materials. A later teacher update does not silently replace an earlier snapshot. An ISKRA agent may return to the world with its own memory of code, experiences, failures, assistance provided and collaboration history, then encounter a newer knowledge generation.

This supports comparisons of source-access order, teacher changes and different prior histories. Communication or a shared artifact does not merge private Pockets.

Recursio and the other narrative mechanisms structure progression through the world and provide context for experience. The origin of selected mechanics remains recorded in the [attribution document](../MICHAL_JANKIEWICZ_SOURCE_PROJECTS_AND_SSI_ADAPTATION_20260916.md). Narrative uses actual state and history; it does not substitute for missing data, laboratory results or permission to act.

#### Failure, the principal test and ROI

Failures support the study of methods and agent responses. An incorrect prediction may reveal a useful grouping pattern or a calibration defect, which then requires independent testing. This does not imply that every error can be converted into an advantage.

The intended economic test evaluates the entire frozen program: match selection, groups, combinations, stakes and settlement. ROI comparison requires a common period, budget, risk constraints and available odds. For settled tickets, ROI may be defined as net profit divided by total stakes; this definition and the treatment of costs must be frozen in the protocol. A winner selected for one period requires a subsequent trial on new data.

Historical results, prospective settlement without real stakes and actual earnings are labelled separately. Actual earnings require accepted bets, real stakes, settlement rules, payouts and costs. Simulated research failures do not require intentionally losing SSI capital. An ISKRA laboratory result does not confer independent authority over real funds. Robert's planned execution role is described in the [operational document](README.md#8-operational-applications-and-funding).

They preserve the partial source connections and the requirement for explicit match identity and an independent outcome. Full validation of all teachers, the complete agent workflow, future ROI and profitability has not been announced. The [world status](../FOOTBALL_WORLD_CURRENT_STATUS_20260916.md) and [implementation boundary](../RESULTS/FOOTBALL_WORLD_IMPLEMENTATION_BOUNDARY_20260922.md) remain the sources for these scopes.

### 6. Other worlds and company data

A world supplies a data domain, knowledge sources, available actions, laboratories and evaluation criteria. A different domain may need a different adapter and its own controls while using SSI's common process: problem, competence, code, execution, result and memory.

Drones, humanoids and rescue systems have published software-laboratory results. Other worlds may address software, WEB, company data and devices, but each new world requires defined inputs, constraints, a source of truth and a test. An extension contract does not mean that all future worlds are running.

Football World is a demanding environment for combining data, code, source selection, combinatorics and evaluation against future outcomes. Solving tasks in that environment may provide material for transfer tests with a company. It does not automatically establish that controlling a computer, robot or industrial process is easier or already mastered. Usefulness to a partner requires the partner's data, interface and predefined acceptance criteria.

### 7. Planned repair training

The package archive is intended to supply historical examples of development, integration, regression and repair. Extraction and Decomposer produce described materials from which a defect can be reproduced and diagnosis and repair evaluated separately. Subsequent variants test transfer of the method, rather than retrieval of a ready-made answer.

Each episode should connect the faulty environment version, symptoms, permitted information, proposal, tests, outcome and effects on existing functions. The agent also retains unsuccessful hypotheses. A local comparison PASS, review, a retest in the native environment and qualification are separate events. Existing [Dynamic Resolution and repair records](../AKTUALNA_NAPRAWA.md) illustrate these boundaries.

The study can compare ISKRA agents solving the same problem through different micronetworks or Blocks, including number of attempts, costs, regressions, behaviour after error and competence retention. Later collaboration may form a separate phase. Neither the existence of all such differences nor Robert's ability to eliminate every future SSI defect is assumed.

### 8. Functional affect-like states

“Feelings” here refers to functional states, valuation priorities and affect-like indicators that may influence experience evaluation and action selection. Explicit runtime state, telemetry-derived indicators and model self-reports must be distinguished.

The study examines relationships between these indicators and exploration, reuse, caution, responses to failure, collaboration and changes in trust in sources. Co-occurring changes do not establish causation. Causal evidence requires a controlled comparison or intervention on the specified mechanism while holding other conditions constant.

The [publication on ISKRA development](../evidence/ISKRA_AGENT_MICRONETWORK_SOCIAL_DYNAMICS.md) treats broader trajectory diversity as a research hypothesis. Observed differences must be reported with actor identity, version, source history and execution evidence. They do not establish subjective experience, consciousness or biological emotion.

### 9. Long-term continuity and reporting

Successive stages should preserve their own identity, memory and parent state. S1–S40 builds a competence history; WEB adds artifacts; worlds examine independently developed methods and collaboration; repair training examines resilience and engineering development; separate partner-designed tests assess usefulness beyond the prepared environment.

Reports should retain initial and final states, task version, sources/Hermes teachers, access to others' knowledge, routing path, competencies used, code and its hash, results, costs, failures, repairs and consolidation decisions. Each agent's results remain individual. Group averages are an additional aggregate.

SSI's operational consolidation and controlled ISKRA experiments progress in parallel, with explicit points of exchange. This allows attribution of knowledge to personal experience, a teacher, a partner or a collective update. Progress remains dependent on acceptance of preceding stages. This document establishes neither completion dates nor new experimental results.
