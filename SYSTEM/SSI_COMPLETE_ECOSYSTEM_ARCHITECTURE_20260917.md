# SSI V5 — Complete Ecosystem Architecture

**Current architecture statement:** 2026-09-17  
**Role:** system-level description of SSI as a complete closed software ecosystem  
**Public boundary:** architecture, evidence and verified behavior only; proprietary implementation remains private.

## 1. SSI is not only an S1-S10 training system

The S1-S10 curriculum is only one controlled process inside a larger SSI ecosystem.

The complete system is organized as a persistent multi-agent software environment containing:

```text

+

+
EVIDENCE / CHECKPOINT / PROVENANCE LAYER
```

The seven BODY lines form independent competence-development paths. DIRECTOR sits above the operational structure and is intended to manage organization-level decisions, tasks, resources, evidence and world/domain activity.

## 2. DIRECTOR — organizational intelligence layer

DIRECTOR is not another training BODY. Its system role is management and orchestration.

Its responsibilities include:

- maintaining organizational state;
- observing BODY_FROZEN and the six ISKRA lines;
- managing missions and priorities;
- consuming evidence rather than silently rewriting it;
- selecting when deeper comparison or consolidation is needed;
- tracking competence availability;
- coordinating versioned upgrade paths;
- enforcing local-control versus public-observer boundaries;
- interacting with domain/world modules;
- preserving organizational continuity through CONTINUUM and Hermes.

In the intended full architecture, DIRECTOR becomes the decision layer connecting the SSI competence ecosystem with external or simulated worlds.

## 3. BODY_FROZEN — reference and consolidation target

BODY_FROZEN is the central stable BODY line.

Its role includes:

- preserving an immutable/reference lineage;
- acting as a controlled comparison target;
- receiving only validated/promoted competence at declared gates;
- executing later training stages after verified consolidation;
- serving as the main post-consolidation BODY for domain experiments;
- retaining rollback and provenance evidence.

The completed V4 run shows BODY_FROZEN progressing through S2-S10 after the S1 ISKRA-only phase and completing the final S10 promotion/regression sequence.

## 4. Six ISKRA lines — independent developmental histories

SSI contains six ISKRA BODY lines:

```text
ISKRA1
ISKRA2
ISKRA3
ISKRA4
ISKRA5
ISKRA6
```

They are not merely six copies used for parallel speed.

Their research purpose is to preserve separate learning histories and produce alternative competence paths that can later be compared by S10.

The architecture therefore separates:

```text
LOCAL LEARNING
!=
CROSS-LINE CONSOLIDATION
```

An ISKRA may develop competence locally without that competence automatically appearing in the other BODY lines. Transfer occurs through declared evaluation/consolidation gates.

## 5. Hermes — persistent competence and memory layer

Hermes is the memory/identity layer used to retain and compare competence state.

Its role includes:

- competence identity;
- provenance;
- source tracking;
- retained positive and negative experience;
- version-aware memory;
- incremental state synchronization where supported by the variant;
- supplying S10 with persistent evidence-backed competence representations.

Hermes allows SSI to treat a learned result as persistent system state rather than as a temporary response from a stateless model call.

## 6. CONTINUUM — persistent operational continuity

CONTINUUM is the persistent mission/state/checkpoint layer.

It exists so that the ecosystem can maintain continuity across runtime restarts and long-running work.

Its scope includes:

- missions;
- checkpoints;
- evidence references;
- current state;
- progress;
- recovery information;
- long-running operational context.

In the completed V4 post-S10 provenance snapshot, the promoted BODY_FROZEN CONTINUUM state was hash-identified together with micronetwork candidates and artifacts.

## 7. Micronetworks, LEGO and Pocket

SSI does not treat every problem as one monolithic model invocation.

The competence layer is decomposed into reusable pieces:

```text
EXPERIENCE
-> MICRONETWORK CANDIDATE
-> VALIDATION
-> REUSABLE COMPETENCE
-> LEGO COMPOSITION / ROUTING
-> CHALLENGER / CHAMPION / SPECIALIST / HOLD
```

Pocket / LEGO Box acts as a catalog/composition layer for reusable competence packages.

This allows later tasks to select, combine, verify or reject earlier competence instead of blindly recomputing everything.

## 8. Router V10 — within-BODY routing

Router V10 is the task/competence routing layer inside the operational flow.

Its role is to choose among paths such as:

- reuse;
- partial/verification flow;
- deeper/full reasoning flow;
- recovery/fallback;
- composition of competence;
- state/history-aware routing.

Different SSI versions preserve different execution/control implementations around this broad role.

## 9. Router S10 — cross-line competence comparison

Router S10 operates at the cross-BODY level.

Its role includes:

- collecting competence from independent histories;
- grouping related competence into families;
- performing fast and/or deep comparison;
- ranking evidence;
- proposing Champion / Challenger / Specialist / Hold outcomes;
- preparing controlled consolidation;
- preserving provenance;
- generating versioned descendants;
- allowing target-specific retesting before acceptance.

The V1-V4 lines are primarily alternative engineering approaches around this broader comparison/control problem.

## 10. S1-S10 — one training process inside the ecosystem

The S1-S10 programme is a controlled curriculum used to develop and compare the BODY lines.

```text
S1  Foundation
S2  LEGO + Micronetwork + Routing Practicum
S3  Applied Autonomy
S4  Robustness / Transfer Preparation
S5  Pre-Experiment Maturity Gate
S6  Multi-Path Equivalence
S7  Counterfactual Rerouting
S8  Cross-Domain Abstraction
S9  Novel Recombination
S10 Generalization Readiness Gate
```

The key point is:

```text
S1-S10 = TRAINING / VALIDATION PROCESS
SSI = COMPLETE PERSISTENT ECOSYSTEM
```

Training modifies competence state, but the system also contains runtime operation, memory, mission control, world interaction, evidence management, consolidation, rollback, observation and domain execution.

## 11. Closed competence lifecycle

The ecosystem is designed as a closed evidence-backed competence loop:

```text
WORLD / TASK
-> DIRECTOR / MISSION
-> BODY / ISKRA
-> ROUTER V10
-> EXECUTION
-> OUTCOME
-> EXPERIENCE
-> MICRONETWORK
-> HERMES
-> S10 CROSS-LINE COMPARISON
-> CHAMPION / CHALLENGER / SPECIALIST / HOLD
-> VERSIONED PROMOTION
-> TARGET RETEST
-> ACCEPT / HOLD / REJECT / ROLLBACK
-> CONTINUUM / EVIDENCE
-> NEXT MISSION
```

This is the central architectural idea of SSI: competence is created, tested, retained, compared, reused, promoted or rejected inside one persistent loop.

## 12. Worlds and domain layers

SSI is designed to operate against multiple world/domain adapters rather than being tied to one benchmark.

Current public evidence contains software-domain work for:

- drone/swarm scenarios;
- humanoid motion/stability scenarios;
- football-world work and world-model infrastructure preserved elsewhere in the repository.

The world/domain layer is where the abstract competence system is exposed to concrete environments.

## 13. Financial World — economic/market operating domain

The architecture also includes a financial/economic world as a target operating domain for DIRECTOR.

At the system-design level, the Financial World is intended to expose structured market/economic state to SSI and to allow DIRECTOR to evaluate decisions using the same evidence, routing, memory and risk-control machinery used elsewhere.

The domain can conceptually include:

```text
MARKET DATA / ECONOMIC STATE
-> STATE REPRESENTATION
-> DIRECTOR
-> V10 / COMPETENCE ROUTING
-> MULTIPLE CANDIDATE STRATEGIES
-> EVIDENCE / RISK / UNCERTAINTY CHECK
-> DECISION OR HOLD
-> OBSERVED OUTCOME

```

Relevant target areas may include market analysis, capital allocation, portfolio/investment simulations and other financial decision environments.

### Important current boundary

The existence of the Financial World in the ecosystem architecture must not be confused with a claim that public evidence currently proves live bank, brokerage, crypto-exchange, Forex or betting-account execution.

The current public repository does **not** claim such live account integrations as validated production connections.

Therefore the accurate statement is:

```text
FINANCIAL WORLD = ecosystem/domain architecture and target operating environment
LIVE EXTERNAL FINANCIAL ACCOUNT EXECUTION = not established by current public evidence
```

This distinction preserves the architecture while keeping the evidence boundary honest.

## 14. Public observer vs local control

The ecosystem has a deliberate authority split.

```text
LOCAL ROOT
= execution authority
= mission/control actions
= runtime changes
= promotion / recovery operations

PUBLIC / REVIEWER INTERFACE
= sanitized read-only observation
= evidence/status inspection
= no arbitrary ROOT execution
= no hidden configuration writes
```

The governing principle is:

```text
OBSERVE != CONTROL
```

The current evidence supports describing SSI as a persistent multi-agent software ecosystem with seven BODY lines, organizational control, memory, routing, consolidation, domain experiments and evidence preservation.

It does not by itself establish:

- physical drone validation;
- physical humanoid validation;
- live autonomous financial trading;
- live bank/brokerage account control;
- independent external replication;
- universal superiority over other AI architectures;
- production readiness;
- AGI or consciousness.

The strongest accurate description is therefore:

> **SSI is a persistent, evidence-oriented multi-agent software ecosystem in which DIRECTOR, BODY_FROZEN, six independent ISKRA lines, Hermes, CONTINUUM, Router V10, Router S10, Micronetwork/LEGO competence and world/domain layers form one closed competence-development and operational loop. S1-S10 is one controlled training programme inside that ecosystem, not the whole system.**
