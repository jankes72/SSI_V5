# SSI V5 — LEGO Pocket, META-LEGO and Director-connected laboratories

**Public update:** 2026-09-22  
**Author / project owner:** Paweł Jankiewicz  
**Scope:** implemented private software architecture and the evidence available for it.

This document describes four connected areas: LEGO Pocket, META-LEGO, the
laboratory services available to the independent DIRECTOR, and ŚWIAT PIŁKI —
HIPNOZA. The proprietary source, resource archives, private configuration,
model prompts and operational access remain private.

## LEGO Pocket and qualified reuse

Raw acquired resources remain in the original Pocket. Prepared executable
packages occupy a separate derived layer. Qualification carries the package's
input/output contract, relevant feature and execution compatibility, provenance,
recorded tests and applicable use conditions.

Discovery and metadata matching select candidates. Execution requires the
applicable qualification, target compatibility and lifecycle gates. A package
can remain unbound or unknown when supporting evidence is absent. Candidate,
Challenger, Champion, Hold/Reject, revocation and rollback retain their distinct
roles; publication of a catalog entry does not promote it.

The latest continuation preflight reported `qualified=1` and `actors=7` without
model calls during that check. This is a readiness check on a qualified package,
not evidence that every raw Pocket resource has been executed or validated.
The resource totals in the dated 2026-09-20 report remain historical audit
figures; this update does not claim a fresh full-catalog census.

## META-LEGO

META-LEGO preserves canonical LEGO identities and adds separately recorded
metadata for routing, contract compatibility, evidence status and structural
checks. Missing evidence remains explicitly unknown.

A recent repair aligned the established `modular_plan` / `lego_plan` names
across author and reviewer views. The required plan and other requirements
remain mandatory. The correction does not invent a plan, relax a verifier
verdict or turn an earlier INCONCLUSIVE into PASS. The new execution must
produce its own passing native and independent CI results.

## R&D execution and unresolved cases

The R&D comparison engine executes a bounded graph against an explicitly
identified reference or approved test vectors. It records observed outputs,
repeatability and input-preservation checks. This is an executed software
comparison within the benchmark's scope.

Training now records case-local unresolved results and their reasons, then
continues eligible independent cases. Genuine FAIL/BLOCKED outcomes, exhausted
budgets, unavailable services, deadlines and unknown transport outcomes retain
stop behavior. A stage requiring an unpassed predecessor is deferred without
new model calls for that stage. INCONCLUSIVE remains INCONCLUSIVE, and incomplete
stages are not published as accepted competence.

## Director-connected controlled experiments

The permanent experiment registry records a protocol before execution:
hypothesis, requirements, domain, baseline, candidate, seed and repetitions.
A changed protocol becomes a new experiment. Results, expert reviews and
permission to deploy are distinct facts.

The current implementation supports bounded data comparisons and a small
2-D point-mass UAV comparison bench. The latter checks declared software
properties such as speed, separation, boundary behavior and loss of commands.
It is separate from the earlier Dual Mother research harness and does not
reproduce its published measurements or qualify a physical autopilot.

BODY_FROZEN can execute a registered experiment through its authorized native
mission path with the existing local Hermes supervisor. The DIRECTOR can
request that execution and consume laboratory metadata while keeping its own
runtime, memory and lifecycle. Training and native experiment execution cannot
concurrently modify the same BODY state. A separately selected standalone CPU
test is reported as such.

The existing collective evidence path remains. New experiment summaries are
available to the seven BODY lines and DIRECTOR; this does not copy private
expert solutions into training prompts, merge agent memories or change neural
weights automatically. Transfer remains evidence- and target-dependent.

## Expert participation and access

The public repository and public observer presentation remain read-only with
respect to SSI execution. Private laboratory access can be issued by the local
owner for a specific role. Observers inspect; authorized experts can additionally
attach a review, request a stop of an active software experiment and provide a
presence heartbeat. They cannot launch arbitrary missions, change a frozen
protocol, alter training grades or obtain ROOT authority through the panel.

Reviews retain their declared conclusion and reasons, tied to the protocol
and result. Locally issued credentials identify access; they do not independently
prove a reviewer's affiliation or independence. The audit hashes establish local
consistency, not third-party certification. Actual external replication remains
a separate milestone.

No operational endpoint, key or installer is published here. A physical vehicle
adapter and a demonstrated physical emergency-stop path are outside the evidence
of this update. The software STOP control is scoped to its experiment executor.

## Four-domain research continuity

The established showcase families remain **DRONES**, **HUMANOID**, **DRONE MOTHER**,
and **DRONE + HUMANOID MOTHER / RESCUE COLLECTIVE**. The dated Dual Mother result
specifically used specialist non-humanoid rescue robots; that measured scope is
preserved.

The new generic laboratory provides configurable domain registration and
experiment protocols. This release is not evidence that four complete new
physical domain models have been built or validated. Domain-specific external
challenges still require their own declared inputs, baselines and criteria.

## ŚWIAT PIŁKI — HIPNOZA

The Football World is Paweł Jankiewicz's existing project line. Its documented
architecture includes versioned T17/Hermes knowledge, agent-specific Pocket,
SOLO/SHARED laboratories, grouping, allocation, decision freezing and separate
outcome evaluation. The latest S11 LEGO results do not validate football data
ingress, match identity, predictions, results or profitability.

The architecture retains independent DIRECTOR and BODY cores. DIRECTOR-led
entry of selected ISKRA agents is an architectural requirement; this update
adds no new live evidence that the entire agent-to-world path has closed.
