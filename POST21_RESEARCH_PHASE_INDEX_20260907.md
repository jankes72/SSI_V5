## What is predeclared now

The following is part of the research plan before Phase-1 results are known:

- post-21 competence inventory;
- semantic/functional deduplication of dynamically named micronetworks;
- Champion enrichment;
- Challenger enrichment and evidence-gated promotion;
- creation of new micronetworks only for genuinely new functional domains;
- preservation of provenance across merged competence;
- aggregate accounting of total skills, unique skills, duplicates, Champion/Challenger changes and new micronetworks;
- creation of a versioned consolidated BODY descendant while preserving BODY_FROZEN T0 as the historical control;
- three additional self-selected transfer experiments in domains not used anywhere in the original 21;
- a before/after comparison intended to measure whether consolidation improves transfer.
