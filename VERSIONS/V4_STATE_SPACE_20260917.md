## DIRECTOR

DIRECTOR is the organization/management layer. Its intended role is to coordinate missions, system priorities, evidence, competence state, domain/world interaction and versioned decisions across the ecosystem.

It is separate from the seven BODY runtime lines and should not be described as another ISKRA.
